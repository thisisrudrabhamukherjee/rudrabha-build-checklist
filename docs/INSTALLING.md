# Installing Rudrabha Mukherjee's Build Checklist

You can install this page on your phone, tablet or computer so it opens like an app, works offline, and keeps your checklist on the device.

Open the live address in a browser: https://rudrabha-build-checklist.pages.dev

A file you saved to disk also works offline, but it cannot be installed as an app and does not receive updates.

## What installing does

- Puts a shortcut on your home screen, taskbar or app list
- Opens in its own window, without the browser's address bar
- Caches the page so it works without an internet connection
- Keeps your work in this browser's storage on this device

Installing does **not** upload your work anywhere. The app never sends what you write over the network.

## Android

1. Open the live address in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the menu (three dots), then **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
3. Confirm. The icon appears on your home screen.

## iPhone and iPad

1. Open the live address in **Safari** (installation from other browsers is limited on iOS).
2. Tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Confirm the name and tap **Add**.

### Storage on iOS

Safari may delete script-writable storage (including `localStorage`) after seven days without interaction. See [WebKit tracking prevention](https://webkit.org/tracking-prevention/) and [Updates to storage policy](https://webkit.org/blog/14403/updates-to-storage-policy/).

**Home Screen apps opened in standalone mode are exempt** from that seven-day rule.

If you use this checklist on iOS:

- Install it to your Home Screen rather than relying on a Safari tab.
- Use **Move or back up my work** inside the app to keep a file copy.
- An installed copy may **not share storage** with Safari. If you used the page in Safari first and then installed it, use the move file to carry your work across.

## Windows

1. Open the live address in **Edge** or **Chrome**.
2. In Edge: click the **App available** icon in the address bar, or use the menu **Apps** → **Install this site as an app**.
3. In Chrome: use the menu **Install Rudrabha Mukherjee's Build Checklist** (or **Install app**).

## macOS

1. Open the live address in **Safari**, **Chrome** or **Edge**.
2. In Safari: **File** → **Add to Dock** (macOS Sonoma or later), or Share → **Add to Dock**.
3. In Chrome or Edge: use **Install** in the address bar or menu.

## Ubuntu and Linux

1. Open the live address in **Chrome** or **Chromium**.
2. Use the menu **Install Rudrabha Mukherjee's Build Checklist** (or the install icon in the address bar).
3. The app appears in your applications menu.

## Storage

Your ticks, notes and settings are kept in the browser on this device, under the address you installed from. They are not uploaded.

- Clearing browser data for this site removes your work.
- A different device or browser starts empty unless you use **Move or back up my work**.

## Updates

When a newer version is published, the app may ask the host whether an update exists (you can switch this off in **Settings**). If an update is ready, a banner appears and **your work is not affected** — you choose when to update.

Your browser may also check the worker file on its own; that cannot be turned off from inside a web page.

A file saved to disk does not update automatically. Download a fresh copy from the live address or from `releases/` when you want the latest version.

## Uninstalling

- **Android:** long-press the icon → **Uninstall**, or remove it from **Settings** → **Apps**.
- **iPhone and iPad:** long-press the icon → **Remove App**.
- **Windows:** **Settings** → **Apps** → find the app → **Uninstall**.
- **macOS:** drag the app from the Dock or Applications folder to the Bin.
- **Linux:** remove it from your applications menu or desktop environment's app list.

Uninstalling removes the installed copy and its cached files. Your work in browser storage may also be removed. Use **Move or back up my work** first if you want to keep it.
